01 The problem

Clipping pays per view.
Rejections pay nothing.

Content Rewards campaigns pay per 1,000 views, but only on posts that follow the brief. Most clippers lose on one of three fronts.

Time

Hours per episode

Scrubbing a 3-hour podcast for ten good moments, then cropping and captioning each one, is most of a day. Volume is the whole game and the day is the cap.

Rejections

Posts that don't count

Mid-sentence openers, the speaker off screen, a sponsor read, a cut in the middle. The campaign rejects it, the views don't pay, and you find out a week later.

Cost

Cloud clippers eat the margin

Subscription clippers charge $20–60 a month and cap your minutes. They also don't read the brief, so the rejection problem stays.

08 FAQ

Questions, answered.

Will it work on my computer?

FLIC runs on Macs with Apple Silicon (M1, M2, M3, M4) on macOS 14 or newer, with about 20 GB free for the speech and language models. Intel Macs and Windows aren't supported yet; a Windows version for NVIDIA cards is on the list.

How many clips do I get, and how long does it take?

Up to 10 per episode. A 3-hour podcast takes under 30 minutes on an M-series Mac, most of it transcription; a 30-minute video takes a few minutes. Keep the Mac plugged in with the lid open while it runs.

Which campaigns does it work with?

Podcast, interview and talking-head clipping campaigns where you post real moments with captions. Paste the brief into ChatGPT with FLIC's prompt: GO means it fits, RISKY means there's a rule you should decide on (a warm-up period, "no clipping tools"), SKIP means the campaign wants a special look or beat-edits that FLIC doesn't make.

Does it guarantee my posts get approved?

No. FLIC enforces the rules that can be checked automatically: openers, unbroken passages, face on screen, sponsor reads, length, platforms. Approval is still the campaign's call, and the posting rules (keep it up 30 days, no paid boosts, required tags) are yours to follow.

Is it a subscription?

No. Pay once. Lifetime updates, no monthly fee, no API costs, no credits. The only ongoing cost is your Mac's electricity.

Does my video get uploaded anywhere?

No. Transcription, scoring, cropping and captions all run on your Mac. The only thing FLIC sends out is a license check to Whop.

How do I get it after buying?

Whop shows your license key. Download one file from the download page, double-click it, paste the key, and it installs everything: the speech model, the local language model and a Start button on your Desktop. There's a step-by-step guide from purchase to posting.

Refunds?

FLIC is a digital product delivered instantly, so all sales are final. Check the requirements above before buying, and message us in the Whop support chat if anything in setup gives you trouble.
