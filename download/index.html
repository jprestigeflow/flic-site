Install FLIC on your Mac

One file, one double-click. It sets up everything FLIC needs and puts a Start button on your Desktop.

Download Install FLIC.command · Mac

You'll be asked for your license key. It's on your Whop purchase page.

What you need

Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or newer) macOS 14 or newer 20 GB free Internet for the install

How the install goes

  1. Double-click the file. If macOS says it can't open it, right-click → Open → Open.
  2. Paste your license key when it asks.
  3. It may ask for your Mac password once (for Homebrew). Nothing shows while you type; press Return.
  4. Wait 15–40 minutes. It's mostly downloading the AI models (about 7 GB).
  5. The guide opens when it's done. From then on: drop a video and brief.txt into ~/FLIC/input and FLIC does the rest.

Windows

Not yet. FLIC's speech engine runs on the Apple chip; a Windows version needs an NVIDIA graphics card and is on the list.
