FAQ

Questions.

Will it work on my computer?

FLIC runs on Macs with Apple Silicon (M1, M2, M3, M4) on macOS 14 or newer, with about 20 GB free for the speech and language models. Intel Macs and Windows aren't supported yet.

How many clips do I get, and how long does it take?

About 12 per hour of video, never fewer than 10 and up to 40. A 3-hour podcast takes about 45 minutes on an M-series Mac. Keep the Mac plugged in with the lid open while it runs.

Does it guarantee my posts get approved?

No. FLIC enforces the rules that can be checked automatically: openers, unbroken passages, face on screen, sponsor reads, length, platforms. Approval is still the campaign's call, and the posting rules are yours to follow.

Does my video get uploaded anywhere?

No. Transcription, scoring, cropping and captions all run on your Mac. The only thing FLIC sends out is a license check to Whop.

How do I get it after buying?

Whop shows your license key. Download one file, double-click it, paste the key, and it installs everything. There's a step-by-step guide from purchase to posting.

Refunds?

FLIC is a digital product delivered instantly, so all sales are final. Check the requirements above before buying, and message us in the Whop support chat if setup gives you trouble.
